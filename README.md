    <section id="overview">
        <h2>Project Overview</h2>

        <p>
            HYBRID PETCRETE investigates the use of recycled PET
            bottle fibers and recycled coarse aggregates in concrete
            to explore sustainable construction materials.
        </p>

        <p>
            The project focuses on evaluating concrete workability,
            mechanical properties, water absorption, and material
            consistency through laboratory experiments.
        </p>

        <div class="cards">
            <div class="card">

    <section id="objectives">
        <h2>Project Objectives</h2>

        <ul>
            <li>Reduce PET plastic waste through reuse.</li>
            <li>Conserve natural coarse aggregates.</li>
            <li>Evaluate concrete workability and mechanical properties.</li>
            <li>Study compressive strength.</li>
            <li>Investigate split tensile strength.</li>
            <li>Evaluate flexural performance.</li>
            <li>Assess water absorption and material consistency.</li>
        </ul>
    </section>

    <section id="methodology">
        <h2>Proposed Experimental Methodology</h2>

        <ol>
            <li>Collect and clean used PET bottles.</li>
            <li>Process PET bottles into fibers with controlled dimensions.</li>
            <li>Prepare recycled coarse aggregates.</li>
            <li>Condition recycled aggregates before mixing.</li>
            <li>Prepare the selected concrete mixtures.</li>
            <li>Conduct fresh concrete workability tests.</li>
            <li>Conduct hardened concrete performance tests.</li>
            <li>Compare results with conventional concrete.</li>
        </ol>

        <h3>Proposed Concrete Mixture Groups</h3>

        <ul>
            <li>Conventional concrete.</li>
            <li>PET fiber-reinforced concrete.</li>
            <li>Recycled aggregate concrete.</li>
            <li>Hybrid PET fiber-reinforced recycled aggregate concrete.</li>
        </ul>
    </section>
